# FAO (2015) - Soil Organic Carbon: The Hidden Potential

Soil organic carbon (SOC) is a key indicator of soil health. Agricultural soils
in semi-arid regions typically hold 0.3-1.0% SOC. Adding legume cover crops in
rotation can raise SOC by 15-25% over 2-3 years through rhizodeposition and
residue return. Conservation tillage reduces oxidative SOC losses by 20-40%
versus conventional tillage.
