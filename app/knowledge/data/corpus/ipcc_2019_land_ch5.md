# IPCC (2019) - Climate Change and Land, Ch. 5

Agroforestry and alley cropping are among the highest-confidence land-based
mitigation options. Systems combining N-fixing trees with annual crops accrue
0.2-0.5% absolute SOC over 5-10 years and simultaneously raise on-farm
biodiversity by creating structural habitat. Riparian buffers reduce nitrogen
runoff by 30-60% (Mayer et al. 2007).
